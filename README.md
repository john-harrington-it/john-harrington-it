# Hi, I'm John Harrington 👋

**Systems Engineer** with 15+ years running enterprise infrastructure for law firms and financial-services organizations. Based in the Baton Rouge, LA area and open to remote, hybrid, or on-site roles.

For more than ten years I was the go-to escalation engineer at a multi-site national law firm: I owned hybrid identity end to end, led Exchange migrations from 2010 to 2016 to 2019 and into hybrid with Exchange Online, and ran a high-availability Citrix, VMware, HPE Nimble, and Veeam stack with 24x7 on-call.

- **Citrix & virtualization**: XenApp/XenDesktop on PVS, StoreFront/NetScaler, VMware vCenter/ESXi, HPE Nimble, Veeam
